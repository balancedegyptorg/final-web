How It Works

One Curriculum, Delivered Locally

FWESS runs on a simple idea: keep the curriculum consistent everywhere, and let local partners decide how best to teach it.

01

Balanced Egypt: Global Coordinator

We design the core curriculum, the learning goals, and the way impact gets measured, so every chapter is teaching toward the same outcomes.

02

Country Lead NGOs

In each country, a lead partner adapts the curriculum to local context, supervises delivery, and consolidates results before they come back to us.

03

Local & Partner NGOs

On the ground, local partners bring it into classrooms however works best for their students, workshops, theatre, games, school gardens, or all of it.

The Curriculum

What Students Learn

01

Food, Value, & Responsibility

Where food comes from, why it matters, and responsible habits students can build.

02

Understanding Food Waste

The difference between food waste and everyday scraps, and why waste happens.

03

Impacts of Food Waste

Who wasted food actually affects, from families to the environment.

04

Reducing Food Waste

Portioning, storage, and creative ways to use leftovers instead of losing them.

05

Community Action

Turning individual habits into something a whole classroom can do together.
