Contact & Role

Founder & Chairperson

m.naguib@balancedegypt.org

Mohamed Naguib is a youth advocate and community leader reshaping how sustainable development and food waste governance are approached at both local and international levels.

As the Founder and Chairperson of Balanced Egypt, Mohamed directs a youth-led initiative focused on tackling food waste through research-backed education, community action, and structured youth leadership. Under his guidance, the organization establishes strategic partnerships with leading academic and governmental institutions to build measurable, data-driven sustainability programs.

Global Advocacy & Institutional Leadership

On the international stage, Mohamed has actively represented youth perspectives across premier multilateral platforms. He served as Egypt's National Delegate and as a member of the international Steering Committee for the 14th UNESCO Youth Forum in Samarkand, Uzbekistan, where he was chosen by the Secretariat to deliver the official closing address on behalf of global delegates.

Deepening his multilateral engagement, Mohamed has played an instrumental role in the UNESCO80 reform roadmap, contributing directly to Pillar II mandate reviews and youth-led reflections. He serves as Youth Focal Point, a key contributor to the Young People's Reflection Document and the Call for a New Culture of Peace, which shaped youth consultations across 135 Member States. His work culminated in the presentation of these outcomes at high-level sessions in Paris alongside global leaders, leading to the formal welcoming of the youth recommendations by UNESCO's Executive Board at its 225th session.

Vision & Impact

"Young people are usually asked to talk about the future. We wanted to actually build it, starting with something as overlooked as the food we waste every day."

Through dedicated community engagement, educational programming, and international policy advocacy, Mohamed champions the integration of environmental responsibility with the broader realities of community development.
