Egyptian Urban Forum

Hosted With

Ministry of Culture, Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, and ASRT

Location

Overview

In August 2025, Balanced Egypt took part in the Egyptian Urban Forum at Maison d'Architecture Égyptienne, alongside the Ministry of Culture, the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, the Academy of Scientific Research and Technology, and several other NGOs. Spanning several days, the program focused on teaching children how food waste connects to the sustainability of the cities they live in.

The Challenge

Children often do not see how something as ordinary as wasted food connects to the bigger systems that keep a city running. Environmental education for children is rarely built on a solid scientific or institutional foundation, and traditional programs almost never use digital storytelling or AI to make the subject engaging. On top of that, young people remain underrepresented in civic sustainability initiatives, which limits how much lasting behavior change these programs can actually create.

Our Approach

We built sessions around storytelling suited to different ages, brought to life with AI generated videos that illustrated real world impacts on cities. Interactive games and hands on activities gave children a way to think critically about sustainability rather than just listen to a lecture, and the whole program was developed alongside government ministries and NGOs to keep it grounded in real institutional relevance.

What We Measured

Children who took part left with a noticeably stronger grasp of how their everyday choices about food connect to the health of the cities around them.

Looking Ahead

The Egyptian Urban Forum confirmed that combining storytelling with technology like AI generated video can make a subject as dry as urban sustainability genuinely click for children. We're looking to bring this same format to future forums and ministry partnerships, and to keep proving that youth led organizations belong at the table in national conversations about the cities of the future.

A youth led Egyptian NGO working to end food waste through research, education, and community action.
