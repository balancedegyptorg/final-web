Overview

In April 2025, Balanced Egypt partnered with Cairo University and the Cairo University Green Office to host an educational initiative on waste management and sustainable development. The program targeted both students and faculty, combining informational talks with interactive booths to create a hands on, participatory learning experience on campus.

The Challenge

University campuses generate a significant amount of food waste, yet most lack any structured awareness campaign around it. Students and faculty often have limited knowledge of sustainable practices or how their daily choices connect to the wider food system, and opportunities for experiential, hands on learning about sustainability remain underused at the university level.

Our Approach

We hosted informational talks that introduced key concepts in food waste management and sustainable development, paired with interactive booths where students and faculty could engage directly with activities demonstrating the real impact of food waste. Every activity offered practical strategies that people could apply on campus and at home, and the initiative was built as an ongoing institutional partnership rather than a one off event.

What We Achieved

The program introduced sustainable practices to both students and faculty, and gave many of them their first real look at Balanced Egypt's wider work. It fostered a stronger culture of responsibility on campus, encouraging students to see themselves as change agents in their own communities, and marked the start of an ongoing relationship between Balanced Egypt and one of Egypt's leading universities. This initiative is part of our broader commitment to embedding food waste awareness within Egypt's higher education institutions.

Looking Ahead

This partnership showed us how much appetite there is for practical sustainability education inside Egyptian universities, from both students and faculty. We're building on it by exploring similar collaborations with other campuses across the country, with the same focus on hands on learning and measurable engagement.
