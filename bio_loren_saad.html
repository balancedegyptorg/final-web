Contact & Role

Public Policy & Partnerships Advisor

l.saad@balancedegypt.org

Loren Emad Morgan is a political science graduate from the American University in Cairo (AUC) with international academic experience at Sciences Po University in Paris, currently pursuing a master’s degree in Comparative and Middle East Politics.

As Public Policy and Partnerships Advisor at Balanced Egypt, Loren contributes extensively to political research, project development, proposal writing, and strategic partnership-building. Her professional background spans engagement with the League of Arab States and civil society initiatives, anchoring her work in public policy, international affairs, and sustainable development.

Background & Motivation

"Growing up in Faiyum, an agricultural city, I witnessed farmers discard tons of crops damaged by extreme heat while food insecurity continued to rise."

Witnessing food loss firsthand in her hometown, coupled with her academic studies in public policy, inspired Loren to join Balanced Egypt. She is driven by the commitment to research food waste as a systemic challenge deeply linked to climate change, food security, and agricultural sustainability.

Key Contributions & Projects

At Balanced Egypt, Loren has played a foundational role in coordinating the Beyond the Bin Camp, alongside developing and facilitating workshops and interactive games on dietary and food consumption behaviors at the World Urban Forum for Children at AUC.

She also enriches the organization's knowledge production through insightful articles, such as her work on Food Waste in Ancient Egypt, while strengthening internal capacities by leading academic workshops on research question formulation, citation practices, and analytical skills.
