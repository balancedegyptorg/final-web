Contact & Role

Chief R&D Officer

m.ibrahim@balancedegypt.org

Minna Ibrahim is a pharmacy student at the Lebanese International University and serves as Chief R&D Officer at Balanced Egypt, coordinating research teams, scientific planning, and evidence-based initiatives.

Her interest in research stems from its powerful potential to address real-world challenges. Minna views food waste as a significant burden carrying environmental, health, economic, and social consequences that deserve rigorous academic attention and action. Alongside her research focus, she strongly believes in the capability of young people to drive meaningful change when provided with the right skills and opportunities.

Research Leadership & Initiatives

At Balanced Egypt, Minna leads the Research and Development team, guiding research planning, formulating core research questions, and supporting the development of evidence-based projects. Through the flagship Beyond the Bin initiative, she has worked closely with young participants exploring food waste implications while helping them cultivate their scientific writing and research competencies.

Vision & Impact

"Food waste is more than discarded food. It is a challenge with environmental and social consequences that deserves greater attention and action."

Through her dedication to research, education, and youth empowerment, Minna continues to shape programs that bridge scientific inquiry with practical, community-driven solutions.
