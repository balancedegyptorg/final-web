Our Story

From a focused question to a wider change.

A youth-led Egyptian initiative working to bring food waste into sharper focus.

How We Started

Balanced Egypt began with a question: why is food waste treated as an everyday inconvenience rather than a core sustainability challenge? Founded by Mohamed Naguib, we grew from the conviction that addressing waste starts by understanding its systemic roots and giving young people a platform to lead.

Our Approach

Through education, public awareness, and research, we turn food waste into an entry point for resource responsibility. Lasting progress requires clear data, practical responses, and informed public dialogue.

Our Vision

An Egypt where food is valued, avoidable waste is challenged, and young people shape a sustainable food system.

Our Mission

To advance food waste understanding through research, education, and local community action.
