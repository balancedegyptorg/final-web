Contact & Role

Chief Communications Officer

m.gomaa@balancedegypt.org

Malak Gomaa is a communications and media practitioner whose work spans digital storytelling, public relations, journalism, and research.

As Chief Communications Officer at Balanced Egypt, Malak oversees the organization's overarching brand narrative, public relations, and digital presence. She designs communication strategies that educate young people on food waste solutions, raise awareness around upcoming workshops and initiatives, and direct campaigns that give audiences compelling reasons to care and clear pathways to act.

Background & Motivation

"Two contradictions stayed with me: seeing edible food thrown away while people struggle, and watching youth told to wait for their moment to lead."

Holding a degree in Political Science from the American University in Cairo (AUC) with a background in political research, Malak joined Balanced Egypt to confront social inequality and prove that young leaders possess the agency to drive meaningful change today rather than waiting for tomorrow.

Strategic Media & External Experience

Malak’s professional background operates at the intersection of development, strategic communications, and journalism. She has contributed research on Youth, Peace and Security at CCCPA, supported NGO fundraising and CSR partnerships at YW Consultancy, and engaged key multilateral and governmental partners including GIZ Egypt, Entlaq, and MSMEDA during her work at MountPR.

Her ongoing work in journalism and multimedia storytelling with Akher Khabar informs her core philosophy at Balanced Egypt: to thoroughly understand complex systemic challenges before telling their story, and to craft narratives that move communities toward practical action.
