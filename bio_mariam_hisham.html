Contact & Role

Chief Projects Officer

m.hisham@balancedegypt.org

Mariam Hisham is a project strategist and community leader driving the implementation of youth-focused sustainability initiatives across Egypt.

As Chief Projects Officer at Balanced Egypt, Mariam oversees the execution of flagship programs from concept to field delivery. She aligns project objectives with organizational goals, coordinates multi-departmental workflows, and ensures every initiative achieves measurable community impact.

Project Execution & Strategy

Mariam leads on-the-ground operational logistics for educational workshops, community action drives, and institutional partnerships. Her leadership ensures that complex initiatives run seamlessly, creating meaningful opportunities for young volunteers and participants to engage directly in food waste reduction solutions.

Vision & Impact

"Lasting environmental progress relies on turning strategic ideas into practical, well-executed community programs that empower young leaders."

Through rigorous project management and dedicated youth mobilization, Mariam continues to expand Balanced Egypt's operational reach and educational presence.
